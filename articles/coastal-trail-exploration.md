# Coastal Trail Exploration

There's something magical about hiking along the coast. The constant sound of waves crashing against the shore, the salty breeze, and the ever-changing landscape create an experience unlike any other.

## The Trailhead

We started our journey at a small parking area near the lighthouse. The trail immediately opened up to reveal stunning ocean views, with waves crashing against the rocky shoreline below. Seagulls circled overhead, their calls mixing with the sound of the surf.

## Hidden Coves

One of the highlights of this trail was discovering several hidden coves that were only accessible at low tide. We timed our hike perfectly, arriving at each cove just as the tide was receding. These secluded spots felt like our own private beaches, complete with tide pools teeming with marine life.

## The Cliffs

The trail wound along the top of dramatic cliffs, offering breathtaking views of the ocean below. At certain points, we could see for miles along the coastline. The contrast between the deep blue of the ocean and the green of the coastal vegetation was striking.

## Sunset Views

We timed our return to coincide with sunset, and we were rewarded with one of the most beautiful sunsets we'd ever witnessed. The sky transformed into a canvas of oranges, purples, and pinks, reflecting off the water below.

This coastal trail reminded us that some of the best adventures are found where land and sea meet.
